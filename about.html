about

all you need to know

Yes, I'm Elio, and I am a student in the UK who has always had an interest in subjects surrounding maths, physics and computer science.

I have been coming up with ideas and have attempted to create them ever since I was a child, and now that I know exactly what I like, I thought that it would be good to have a website where I can store them all. I thought that by doing this, I am able to look back at it in the future to take a look at my journey, and see what I've learnt and how much I've improved.

As of now, I don't have too much knowledge on programming languages, with my strongest being HTML, CSS, and, of course, Python. However over the years, I plan to continuously take on projects involving these as well as other languages in order to try and push myself to make greater, complex things.

However, aside from computer science, maths and physics also play a huge role, and so I also would like to, over the years, take on challenges heavily involving these subjects; specifically my logical thinking.

On this website, coded by me, I have decided to keep a blog to record what I'm doing relating to those three interests. For larger, significant projects, I dedicated a projets page where I will upload images, the process and problems on each individual one.

With all of that said, enjoy your time here!
